YORKSHIRE MAN & VAN REMOVALS — WEBSITE v12
==========================================

DEPLOY (Cloudflare Pages, free plan)
  Upload the CONTENTS of this folder (index.html, css/, js/, assets/, _headers, etc.)
  via Pages > Create > Direct Upload, or connect it to a Git repo.

DOMAIN
  The site is set up for https://yorkshiremanandvanremovals.co.uk (no www) in the canonical link,
  social preview, structured data, robots.txt and sitemap.xml. If you decide to use www
  instead, search and replace "https://yorkshiremanandvanremovals.co.uk" with
  "https://www.yorkshiremanandvanremovals.co.uk" in those files. In Cloudflare, add a redirect from
  whichever version you don't use (Rules > Redirect Rules, free).

AFTER GOING LIVE
  - Add the site to Google Search Console and submit sitemap.xml.
  - Use the same phone number and website on your Google Business Profile.
  - Optional: Cloudflare Web Analytics (free, cookie-free): Pages project > Metrics > Enable.

THINGS TO KEEP AN EYE ON
  - The towns under "Based in Doncaster, working across Yorkshire" (index.html, class="area-list").
  - privacy.html is a plain-English starter notice, not legal advice.

HOW THE FORM WORKS
  3 short steps (what & when / where / what & who). Answers are turned into a WhatsApp message
  that the customer checks and sends. Nothing is stored on a server.
