Privacy notice

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Who we are

Yorkshire Man & Van Removals, covering Doncaster and across Yorkshire. You can contact us on 07359 307175 or via WhatsApp.

What happens when you use the enquiry form

This website does not store your form details on a server. When you press “Continue to WhatsApp”, the form builds a message on your own device and opens WhatsApp so you can review it. Nothing is sent to us until you press Send in WhatsApp.

If you send it, we receive the details you included (such as your name, phone number, addresses, preferred date and item list) and use them only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and arrange the job. WhatsApp is run by Meta and handles messages under its own terms and privacy policy.

Phone calls and messages

If you call or message us directly, we use the details you give us for the same purpose: responding to your enquiry and carrying out any job you book.

Website hosting and analytics

This website is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical information such as your IP address to deliver the site securely. This website does not use advertising cookies. If we switch on Cloudflare Web Analytics, it measures visits without using cookies or tracking individuals across sites.

Links to other sites

Our Facebook, TikTok and WhatsApp links take you to services run by other companies, which have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us using the details above. If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
